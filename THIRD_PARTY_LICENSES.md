# Third-party DSP

## Airwindows ButterComp2 and DeBess

The mono offline dynamics processors in `voice_enh/buttercomp_runner.py` and
`voice_enh/studio_dsp_runner.py` adapt the ButterComp2 and DeBess
signal-processing equations by Chris Johnson / Airwindows.
